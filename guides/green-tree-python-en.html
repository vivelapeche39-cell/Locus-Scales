Green tree python

Quick sourced reference values. LOCUS Scales' full breeding/genetics toolset is built specifically for ball pythons — this page is a courtesy reference for other species.

Scientific name
Morelia viridis
Native range
New Guinea, nearby islands and the northeastern tip of Australia (Cape York Peninsula)
Lifespan
Into the mid-teens with good care, a few into their mid-20s
Lifestyle
🌳 Arboreal (spends the day coiled over a branch, head in the center)
Warm side
30°C
Cool side
26°C
Humidity
55%

Adult: 4-6 ft (1.2-1.8 m). Since 2020, researchers recognize two species: the northern green python (Morelia azurea, north of New Guinea's central mountains) and the southern one (Morelia viridis, down to the Aru Islands and Australia). Hobby "localities" (Aru, Biak, Sorong, Merauke…) name where the ancestors came from; their reliability varies. A display animal rather than a handling snake. Pythons are the most affected by serpentovirus (formerly nidovirus): quarantine every new arrival, in a separate room.

Diet: Carnivore — mice, then small rats, frozen-thawed, offered with tongs in the evening. In the wild it ambushes prey and sometimes wiggles its tail tip like a worm to lure it in.

Feeding frequency: Juvenile: a small mouse every 5-7 days. Older juvenile and young adult: a hopper or medium mouse every 7-10 days. Adult: one or two adult mice, or a small rat, every 10-14 days. Don't overfeed: it barely moves and gains weight easily.

Substrate: Mulch or coconut husk: they help hold humidity, and once dampened they stay moist for several days. The enclosure should still dry out between mistings.

UVB: Not needed for calcium according to Reptiles Magazine. A full-spectrum lamp brings out the colors; 12 hours of light a day on a timer.

🏠 Enclosure size

Baby : 12×12×12in
Sub-adult : 24×24×24in
Adult : 24×24×24in or 36×24×24in — horizontal space matters more than height

Common questions

Can I handle it?
In moderation. It's a display animal: too much handling stresses it, and it may strike if startled while resting. Many tolerate short handling sessions. Never pull it off its branch: let it climb onto a removable perch, or bring your hand in from below.
My baby is yellow (or red), is that normal?
Yes. Babies hatch bright yellow or brick red, sometimes both colors in the same clutch, and turn green around 1 year old (about 22 in). Red babies often go through yellow before green. Some adults keep some yellow, others turn bluish.
What is a "locality"?
The name of the place the animal's ancestors came from: Aru (known as the calmest), Biak (big, often over 6 ft, known for their attitude), Sorong (blue down the back), Merauke, etc. These are hobby names, more or less reliable. Since 2020, researchers also split the northern and southern green pythons into two species.
Why so much emphasis on quarantine?
Because of serpentovirus (formerly nidovirus). It causes pneumonia, pythons are the most affected, and green tree python collections have suffered heavy losses. A snake can carry it while looking perfectly healthy. Quarantine every new arrival in a separate room and ask your vet about testing. Buy captive-bred: many imported animals come from farms and carry parasites.
