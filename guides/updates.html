What's changed recently

A look at the latest app updates — not an exhaustive list of every small fix, just the real news.

September 8, 2026
New: feeder rodent management

If you breed your own mice/rats/ASF, there's now a real section for that — separate from your reptiles. Track your breeding bins, log litters with full detail (born, missing/cannibalism, transferred, euthanized), and see a profitability report that flags your least productive bins. Growth stage now calculates itself based on age, with adjustable thresholds. A monthly report, a link to your feeder stock, and a dedicated budget with ROI calculation round it all out.

August 31, 2026
Racks & Terrariums, improved

Big cleanup in the Racks & Terrariums section. Each terrarium now has its own record: size, status, lighting, heating, substrate. Cleaning tracking (partial or complete) with a reminder if it's been a while, for both racks and terrariums.
