What LOCUS Scales does

A reptile breeding management app, built around ball pythons and expanded to several other species — meant to replace paper/spreadsheet tracking with a complete system, without overselling what it does.

🧬 Genetics

  • Ball python cross calculator — 78 genes, exclusive sources Morphpedia and ReptiDex
  • Allelic complexes modeled (e.g. Spider Complex), bilingual health warnings
  • Automatic detection of unexpected genetic surprises in a baby, with retroactive resolution

🏠 Husbandry

  • Rack and terrarium management — lighting, heating, substrate, cleaning with reminders
  • Round Mode — quick bulk actions (fed, shed, refused, water change) across a whole collection
  • QR code per animal or per whole rack — scanning jumps straight to quick actions
  • Feeding reminders based on each animal's real history, not identical thresholds for everyone

🐣 Breeding

  • Complete pairing tracking — locks, follicle sizing, ovulation, pre-lay shed
  • Support for egg-laying and live birth — the form adapts to the parents' species
  • Goal-based pairing planner

🐭 Feeder rodents

  • Colony tracking separate from reptiles — bins, ratios, litters
  • Loss tracking (cannibalism, transfers, euthanasia), not just births
  • Profitability report that flags your least productive bins
  • Dedicated budget with ROI calculation

💰 Sales and finances

  • Sale statuses (kept/for sale/reserved/sold) with deposit and balance-due tracking
  • Invoice and receipt PDF generation
  • KPI dashboard — collection value, fertility, feeding compliance, profit
  • Exportable reports in Excel and PDF

🔒 Security and privacy

  • Column-level encryption for sensitive customer data
  • Self-service account deletion
  • Fully bilingual French/English interface
Free during the current testing phase

Paid tiers are planned eventually, always announced in advance.
