Species care guide

Chinese cave gecko

Quick sourced reference values. LOCUS Scales' full breeding/genetics toolset is built specifically for ball pythons — this page is a courtesy reference for other species.

Scientific name
Goniurosaurus hainanensis
Native range
China: Hainan Island (humid lowland forests, caves and rocky outcrops)
Lifespan
10 to 12+ years with good care (ReptiFiles)
Lifestyle
🌍 Terrestrial and nocturnal (movable eyelids, doesn't climb glass)
Warm side
27°C
Cool side
23°C
Humidity
65%

Adult: about 8-9 in (20-23 cm). A room-temperature species: warm zone 80-82°F (27-28°C), cool zone 72-77°F, night 68-72°F. Very heat-sensitive: risk of heat stress above 82°F (28°C) ambient. Humidity 55-65% (Reptiles Magazine), lower by day and higher at night. The genus Goniurosaurus has about 13 species; G. hainanensis is the most common in captivity.

Diet: Insectivore: half-grown crickets, young dubia roaches, a few superworms at a time; waxworms only as a treat (very fatty). Insects dusted with calcium with vitamin D3, plus a multivitamin.

Feeding frequency: Young: daily, spacing meals out as it grows. Adult: twice a week (3 times in breeding season) according to Reptiles Magazine, or every other day according to ReptiFiles. Don't feed when the room drops below 70°F (21°C).

Substrate: A moisture-holding mix, 2-3 in deep: for example 60% fertilizer-free topsoil and 40% coconut fiber. No sand-only substrate, no potting soil with fertilizer.

UVB: Reptiles Magazine doesn't use any; ReptiFiles recommends low UVB (UV index 1-2 in the warm area), 12 hours of light a day.

🏠 Enclosure size

Baby : Small terrarium or ventilated tub with humid hides
Sub-adult : Same as adult
Adult : 24×18×18 in for one animal or a pair; 36×18×18 in for up to 4. Floor space matters more than height.

Common questions

Can I handle it?
Not much: it's mostly a display animal, more nervous than a leopard gecko. Wait at least 2 weeks after arrival before handling. It can learn to take food from tongs or fingers.
Does it need a heat lamp?
Often not: it does well at room temperature (around 72°F) and tolerates heat poorly. Above 82°F ambient it risks heat stress. Watch the room in summer.
Can I keep several together?
Never two males together. A female with a male only if you want breeding. Males show a pronounced bulge at the base of the tail.
How is it different from a leopard gecko?
Same family (movable eyelids), but it comes from humid forests: it needs more humidity, less heat and a substrate that holds moisture. It's also smaller and more nervous.
