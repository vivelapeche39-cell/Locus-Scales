Should you feed a ball python in shed?

Your ball python is in blue and it's feeding day. Offer or skip? There's no single rule: it depends on the snake. Here is what keepers do, the one real precaution, and how to learn each animal's habit.

🔵 How to tell a shed is coming

  • A few days before a shed, the colors turn dull, the belly can look pink, then the eyes go milky blue (« in blue »).
  • The eyes clear up a few days before the old skin comes off. That clear phase usually means the shed is close.

🍽 Offer or skip: keepers are split

  • Many keepers simply offer on the normal feeding day, blue or not, and let the snake decide.
  • Others never offer during a shed, because most of their snakes refuse anyway and they don't want to waste a prey item.
  • Both work. Habits differ from one snake to the next: some eat right through every shed, others won't touch food until the old skin is off.

✅ A simple way to learn each snake

  • Offer once or twice while the snake is in blue and watch what happens.
  • If it eats, keep offering during future sheds. If it refuses, skip feeding during sheds and offer again once the skin is off.
  • Write it down each time: after a few sheds, you know each animal's habit instead of guessing.

⚠️ The one real precaution

  • In blue, the eyes are covered and the snake sees poorly. It can strike defensively, at the prey or at your hand.
  • Use tongs, offer frozen-thawed if your snake takes it, and never leave a live rodent alone with a snake, in shed or not.
  • Some keepers avoid feeding in the clear phase, right before the skin comes off: one reports a shed that came off in pieces after a meal at that stage. It's an anecdote, not a rule, but it costs nothing to wait a few days.

🤷 A refusal in shed is not a worry

  • For a healthy adult, one skipped meal during a shed is normal. Offer again after the shed.
  • What matters is the trend: track the weight. A loss over several weeks, outside a shed or breeding season, is worth a closer look, ideally with a reptile vet.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Can I feed my ball python while it is in blue?

Yes, many keepers offer on the normal feeding day and let the snake decide. Some snakes eat during every shed, others refuse every time. Offer once or twice to learn your snake's habit, with tongs, because its vision is reduced.

Is it a problem if my ball python refuses food during a shed?

Not for a healthy adult. A refusal during a shed is common: offer again after the shed and watch the weight trend over several weeks.

Should I offer a live rodent to a snake in shed?

Its vision is reduced, so it is more vulnerable. Never leave a live rodent alone with a snake; frozen-thawed offered with tongs is the safer option if your snake accepts it.
