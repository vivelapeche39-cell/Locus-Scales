Species care guide

Corn snake

Quick sourced reference values. LOCUS Scales' full breeding/genetics toolset is built specifically for ball pythons — this page is a courtesy reference for other species.

Scientific name
Pantherophis guttatus
Native range
Southeastern United States
Lifespan
15-20 years
Lifestyle
🌍🌳 Semi-arboreal (good climber, mostly terrestrial)
Warm side
30°C
Cool side
23°C
Humidity
50%

Classic 40-60% range cited by most sources. Some more recent sources (ReptiFiles, The Bio Dude) argue for 65-75% based on actual native habitat — debate not settled, adjust based on your own observations.

Diet: Carnivore — rodents (mice), frozen-thawed.

Feeding frequency: Baby: weekly. Juvenile: every 7-10 days. Adult: every 10-14 days.

Substrate: Aspen shavings most common, or paper towel/newspaper (easy to monitor). Coconut fiber also works to hold a bit more humidity. Never pine or cedar — toxic oils.

UVB: Long considered unnecessary (mostly crepuscular species), Ferguson zone 1-2 depending on the source — low UVB (2-6%) is now considered beneficial even if not strictly required.

🏠 Enclosure size

Baby : 20×10×12in (8-20in long)
Sub-adult : 36×18×18in (20-48in long)
Adult : 48×18×24in (ideal for adults)

Common questions

Why does everyone talk about escaping with this species?
It's the trait that defines the species — corn snakes are known as real escape artists, able to squeeze through openings that look impossibly small (if the head fits, the whole body follows). Check every seam, lid corner, and any gap around cable pass-throughs before even setting the animal in — it's the first real surprise for a new owner who underestimates how little a poorly secured lid takes.
Can I keep two together?
No — a solitary species, doesn't need company. Cohabiting increases chronic stress and the risk of disease transmission if one carries something without visible symptoms. Always separate enclosures.
How long should I wait after feeding before handling?
24-48 hours minimum — handling too soon after a meal increases the risk of regurgitation, especially in a young, fast-growing animal.
How often should I handle a new corn snake?
Let it settle in for 5-7 days without handling after arrival — time to adjust to its new environment without added stress. After that, short, regular sessions (10-15 min, a few times a week) acclimate the animal better than long, rare ones.
Can I handle it while it's shedding?
Generally not recommended, especially during the "blue" phase when the eyes turn opaque — vision is reduced, which increases the risk of an accidental bite or unnecessary stress. Wait until the shed is complete.
