Commitments

Our commitments

What LOCUS does with your data, and what it doesn't. Each point describes the app as it is today and can be checked. If anything changes, it will be announced ahead of time in the Release notes.

🔒 Your data

  • Hosted in Canada: database, sign-in and photos at Supabase, ca-central-1 region (Montreal).
  • Your customers' data (name, contact details) is encrypted server-side.
  • Public links (shared record, QR, storefront) never show your purchase price, your buyers or where your animals are.
  • You can export everything at any time, for free: full backup, Excel, PDF. You can delete your account yourself.
  • Your data is also kept on your device: LOCUS works offline.

👁 No tracking

  • No visitor analytics or advertising tools: no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no advertising cookies.
  • No downloaded fonts. LOCUS uses the fonts already installed on your device. No request goes to Google Fonts, so your IP address isn't sent there. It's not a detail: in 2022, a German court (Munich) ruled that passing a visitor's IP address to Google Fonts without consent breached the GDPR.
  • The libraries the app uses (Supabase sign-in, reading Excel files) are served by our own site, not by an external service.
  • The Google and Bing tags in the page only prove the site is ours (Search Console). They don't track anyone.

🤝 External services: the full list

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any host, it receives your IP address when you open a page.
  • Supabase stores your data, sign-in and photos, in Canada.
  • Stripe processes payments, only when you pay (subscriptions aren't active yet).
  • External links (MorphMarket, World of Ball Pythons, sources) are only contacted if you tap the link.

📣 No aggressive marketing

  • No paid ads, no fake reviews, no fake engagement.
  • No countdowns or fake urgency to make you pay.
  • Tiers are announced ahead of time. Reliability, offline mode, backup, export and installation stay free.
  • Your data is neither sold nor used for advertising.

🧬 Transparency

  • Genetics: Morphpedia as the main source, World of Ball Pythons as a complement. When something isn't certain, it says so (documented, reported by the community, uncertain).
  • Every visible change is described in the Release notes, in English and French — error fixes too.
  • A single developer, reachable directly in the app (☰ menu › Contact us).
🔍 How to check
On a computer, open the app, then the browser's developer tools (F12) › Network tab, and reload the page. You'll see who LOCUS talks to: our site, Supabase, and nothing else.
